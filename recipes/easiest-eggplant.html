Easiest Eggplant

This is my favorite recipe

Try this easy eggplant recipe once, and you’ll likely make it again and again. You only need eggplant, seasoned bread crumbs, and mayonnaise. Some of the best recipes are the easiest!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup seasoned bread crumbs
  • 1 medium eggplant, peeled and sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 4 tablespoons mayonnaise, or as needed

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. Place bread crumbs in a small shallow dish.
  3. Coat eggplant slices on both sides with mayonnaise then press into bread crumbs to coat.
  4. Place eggplant on the prepared baking sheet.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until the bottoms are golden brown, about 20 minutes.
  6. Flip slices over and continue baking until brown on the other side, 20 to 25 minutes more.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
